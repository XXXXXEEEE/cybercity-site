About the city

Can I join without chip design experience?

Start with a simple challenge to learn the inputs, outputs, and decision rules, then explore the reference solution. Try Access Key on this page. The full design report includes learning materials.

Does adding components always grow the city?

The aim is to expand the city’s capabilities with verified, compatible designs. Copying a component alone does not create progress: facilities also need correct connections, control logic, and execution. The three stages shown here are concept previews, not live construction statistics.

Does “tapeout” here mean making physical chips?

At this stage, tapeout means designing, simulating, and verifying digital circuit netlists. It does not imply that physical silicon chips have been manufactured. The city’s buildings and devices are virtual too.

Can I connect a wallet and start mining?

Not yet. Specifications, reference netlists, and a local verification engine are complete for all 160 challenges. Challenge registration, on-chain verification, and reward contracts still need development, testing, and auditing. The hero’s mint sequence is illustrative, not actual wallet activity. This page has no wallet connection, minting, or payment features; reward contracts are not live.
